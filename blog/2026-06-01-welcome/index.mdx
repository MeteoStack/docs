using a custom self-built weather station.

## Community-driven

I intend this to be a community-driven project, and the community will be involved
throughout every decision possible, polls will be held when making decisions, so
watch out for them, as that's how you can help choose what happens with the project!

If you would like to join in on the decision making, please either join the
[Discord server](https://go.wtdawson.info/ms-ds/), and or the
[Serchat server](https://go.wtdawson.info/ms-sc/). This is where polls are linked to,
and where you can receive the latest news and announcements.

## Little-to-no reliance on third-party dependencies

MeteoStack will run on its own libraries where possible, to minimise the reliance on third-party dependencies,
this is so that it's easier to manage, to update, but also for security, and for the overall quality of the software.
